The boundary

Gutter Math keeps section, seam, downspout and slope arithmetic only. Every figure is a labeled norm, not a promise - roof shape, rainfall and corner cuts change real runs. Gutter work is ladder work at real height: follow the ladder's and the manufacturer's instructions, and call a professional when the height is beyond you. Call 811 before burying any drain.

Seamless is a word printed on a truck; every sectional gutter is a row of seams waiting their turn. Gutter Math does the honest arithmetic: how many sections and seams the eave really takes, how many downspouts the roof really drains into, and how much fall the run really needs.

Three panels. The seam truth counts sections and their seams. The downspout truth divides the roof by the drainage norm. The slope truth turns the slope norm into real inches of fall.
